# Scientific provenance

The distribution contains the Yanayacu results from the corrected reviewer edition of 8 October 2026, packaged on 26 November 2026. The sample cohort and scientific results were unchanged during packaging.

Input files, scripts and execution records are linked through source manifests and hashes. Their dates document the processing history. Earlier complete deliveries, backup archives, confidential reviewer correspondence and private author notes are excluded.

The comparative cohort comprises nine soil units, A1-A3, B1-B3 and M1-M3, measured with both 16S and ITS. J denotes Jaén and SI denotes San Ignacio. P1 remains in the original sequence deposition but is excluded from the comparative analysis.

Recorded soil measurements and prepared analytical values are preserved separately. Particle-size normalization is documented as a computational transformation, not a laboratory correction. Laboratory units and methods remain unverified where primary records were unavailable.

Validation records specify the executed file-identity checks and numerical analyses. These checks do not establish field independence or supply missing laboratory records. A complete new FASTQ-to-figure analysis was not performed.
